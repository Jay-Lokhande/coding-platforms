# Stay safe

## Fake “coding” opportunities

- **Pay for internship** or **pay for certificate** from unknown portal
- **Data entry / captcha** jobs marketed as “remote developer work”
- **Telegram** “LeetCode premium free” that steals login
- **Clone sites** (`leet-code.com`, etc.) — bookmark official domains

## Real vs practice

LeetCode, Codeforces, etc. are **practice** — they are not employers. Jobs come from company careers pages or verified campus drives.
